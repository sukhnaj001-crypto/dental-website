AI Smart Dental Scanner Mouthpiece

A revolutionary wearable healthcare device that automatically scans teeth and gums, detects dental problems using Artificial Intelligence, and generates an instant oral health report.

Explore Project

Problem Statement

Limited Access

Many people cannot regularly visit dentists due to cost and location barriers.

Late Detection

Dental problems often remain unnoticed until they become serious.

Clinic Dependency

Current diagnostic systems require professional equipment and trained operators.

No Continuous Monitoring

People rarely monitor oral health between dental visits.

Key Features

🦷 Wearable Mouthpiece

Comfortable mouthpiece with built-in miniature cameras and sensors.

🤖 AI Analysis

Detects cavities, plaque, gum disease, tooth wear, and oral abnormalities.

📊 Instant Report

Provides detailed oral health reports within minutes.

📱 Mobile App Integration

Stores reports, health history, and recommendations.

📈 Dental Health Score

Simple oral health score to monitor improvements over time.

☁ Cloud Storage

Secure storage and long-term dental health tracking.
